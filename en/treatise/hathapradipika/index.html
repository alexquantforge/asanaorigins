Haṭhapradīpikā: all 15 āsanas — a full study based on the critical edition (Oxford – Marburg, 2024)

Haṭha yoga · the electronic critical edition “Light on Haṭha Yoga” · Oxford – Marburg, 2024 · verses 1.19–1.54 with the frame 1.17–1.18 and 1.55–1.56

Compiled on 12.10.2026 from the electronic critical edition “Light on Haṭha Yoga” (yoga.ames.ox.ac.uk). For every verse: Devanagari, the scholarly transliteration (IAST), the edition’s translation, our own rendering, the metre, and the full apparatus of variant readings; at the end of every section — a word-by-word analysis, literal and literary translations, and then the editors’ commentary (as in the edition, in English). This document contains no sigla: every manuscript is named in full wherever it is mentioned — collection, shelfmark, city; all abbreviations are expanded on the spot, no need to look them up. There is no Cyrillic transliteration in this English version (its twin document, /home/user/hp_asanas_15.md, has one): Sanskrit is given in Devanagari and IAST only.

The edition gives 15 āsanas: svastika, gomukha, vīra, kūrma, kukkuṭa, uttānakūrma, dhanu, matsyendra, paścimatāṇa, mayūra, śava — and four “best” ones: siddha, padma, siṃha, bhadra (verses 1.19–1.54; the frame — 1.17–1.18, the transition to prāṇāyāma — 1.55–1.56).
