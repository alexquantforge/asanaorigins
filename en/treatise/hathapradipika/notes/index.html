Special cases: exactly what differs in the manuscripts

  • 1. Lost leaves in Nepalese-German Manuscript Preservation Project, microfilm B 62-20 (Nepalese microfilm). In this manuscript the leaves are lost in two places of the section at once: verses 1.18–1.28 and 1.39–1.46; at each of them the apparatus notes “leaf lost”. Other manuscripts have omissions too: “zeta-1” lacks verse 1.20; Sarasvatī Bhavan (manuscript number 29899), Vārāṇasī, lacks verse 1.43 (there it stands earlier, before verse 3.32); “pi-1” lacks verse 1.48; Sarasvatī Bhavan (manuscript number 30109), Vārāṇasī, lacks verse 1.56.
  • 2. The manuscript “epsilon-1” has its own list of āsanas. After verse 1.18 it adds two verses with its own enumeration: svastika, gomukha, padma, kūrma, uttānakūrmaka, kukkuṭa, matsyendra, dhanu, paścimatāṇa, mayūra, siddha, siṃha, bhadra, and “the bound lotus” (baddhapatma) — “and so on, many āsanas”. This is the same set of fifteen poses, but in a different order and with different names — a rare manuscript witness to the “canon of the fifteen āsanas”.
  • 3. An additional line in 1.53. Between the quarters 1.53ab and 1.53cd, Nepalese-German Manuscript Preservation Project, microfilm G 25-2 (Nepal, microfilm), the Lalchand Research Library, M-2089, Chandigarh, and the version of Brahmānanda have an extra line: “savyagulphaṃ tathā savye dakṣagulphaṃ tu dakṣiṇe” — it describes the same as the quarter cd.
  • 4. The dynamic dhanurāsana. In Sarasvatī Bhavan (manuscript number 30051), Vārāṇasī, the scribe left a note: dhanurāsana should be practised without interruption (anavarata), alternately to the left and to the right — that is, as a dynamic practice.
  • 5. A conjecture in 1.35. The reading “ekahṛdayo” was introduced by the editors themselves: the manuscripts give “ekahṛdaye”, “eva hṛdaye”, “eva niyataṃ”, “āsyahṛdaye” and other variants; the accepted reading is supported by texts close in tradition (Vivekamārtaṇḍa, Yogacintāmaṇi).
  • 6. Transpositions. Verse 1.17 is transposed with the following one in Sarasvatī Bhavan (manuscript number 30069), Vārāṇasī; Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin (manuscript Orientalia 11056); Sarasvatī Bhavan (manuscript number 30051), Vārāṇasī. And in the manuscript “zeta-1” verse 1.19 stands earlier — before verse 1.17.
  • 7. The structure of “gamma-1” (Nepalese-German Manuscript Preservation Project, microfilm G 25-2). It has its own division into chapters: its “chapter 3” ends with the 100th verse, its “chapter 4” contains 29 verses, and its “chapter 5” corresponds to the ordinary chapter 4. This matters if one checks the numbering against this manuscript.
