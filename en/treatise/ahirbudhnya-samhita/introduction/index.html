Opening and List of Āsanas

Ahirbudhnya-saṃhitā · Chapter 31 · eleven principal āsanas

The rubric preceding the list reads अथासनानां स्वास्थ्यहेतुत्वम् — “Now, on āsanas as a cause of health / well-being.”

आसनानि प्रवक्ष्यामि मुख्यानि विविधानि च ।आस्थायैषामन्यतमं योगिनो यान्ति निर्वृतिम् ॥

āsanāni pravakṣyāmi mukhyāni vividhāni ca ।āsthāyaiṣām anyatamaṃ yogino yānti nirvṛtim ॥

āсанāни правакшйāми мукхйāни вивидхāни ча ।āстхāйаишāм анйатамам̣ йогино йāнти нирвр̣тим ॥

Literal translation

I shall explain the principal and diverse āsanas; having assumed one of them, yogins attain repose.

Literary translation

“I shall explain the principal poses. By assuming one of them, the yogin attains repose.”

List, verses 31–32

चक्रं पद्मासनं कूर्म मायूरं कौक्कुटं तथा ॥३१॥वीरासनं स्वस्तिकं च भद्रं सिंहासनं तथा ।मुक्तासनं गोमुखं च मुख्यान्येतानि नारद ॥३२॥

cakraṃ padmāsanaṃ kūrma māyūraṃ kaukkuṭaṃ tathā ॥31॥vīrāsanaṃ svastikaṃ ca bhadraṃ siṃhāsanaṃ tathā ।muktāsanaṃ gomukhaṃ ca mukhyāny etāni nārada ॥32॥

Translation

Cakra, Padma, Kūrma, Mayūra, and Kukkuṭa; likewise Vīrāsana, Svastika, Bhadra, and Siṃhāsana; Muktāsana and Gomukha—these are the principal [āsanas], O Nārada.
