Appendix. Transition to Prāṇāyāma and the End of the Chapter

Ahirbudhnya-saṃhitā

After the āsanas, the text moves directly to the purification of the nāḍīs.

प्राणायामप्रसिद्ध्यर्थं नाडीशुद्धिमनन्तरम् ।देहस्थवायुभिः कुर्यात्सानिभिश्च तपोधन ॥४७॥

prāṇāyāma-prasiddhyarthaṃ nāḍīśuddhim anantaram ।dehasthavāyubhiḥ kuryāt sānibhiś ca tapodhana ॥47॥

прāн̣āйāмапрасиддхйартхам̣ нāд̣ӣш́уддхим анантарам ।дехастхавāйубхих̣ курйāт сāнибхиш́ ча таподхана ॥47॥

Word-by-word analysis

prāṇāyāma-prasiddhi-artham
for the success of prāṇāyāma
nāḍī-śuddhim
purification of the nāḍīs, the bodily channels
anantaram
after this
deha-stha-vāyubhiḥ
by means of the vāyus (“winds”) within the body
kuryāt
let one perform
sānibhiḥ ca
so printed the reading and meaning are unclear
tapodhana
O ascetic, rich in tapas.

Translation

For success in prāṇāyāma, after purification of the nāḍīs, one should practice by means of the winds residing in the body, … (the reading सानिभिश्च is unclear) … O ascetic.

Colophon

इति श्रीपाञ्चरात्रे तन्त्ररहस्ये अहिर्बुध्न्यसंहितायां

योगाङ्गयमनियमासननिरूपणं नामैकत्रिंशोऽध्यायः । ओम् । (आदितः श्लोकाः १८७२)

iti śrīpāñcarātre tantrarahasye ahirbudhnyasaṃhitāyāṃyogāṅga-yama-niyama-āsana-nirūpaṇaṃ nāma ekatriṃśo 'dhyāyaḥ ।om । (āditaḥ ślokāḥ 1872)

Translation

Thus ends chapter 31 of the Ahirbudhnya-saṃhitā in the Tantrarahasya of the Pāñcarātra, entitled “Exposition of the limbs of yoga, yama, niyama, and āsana.” Om. The printed colophon states: “from the beginning, 1,872 verses.”
