SECTION II. CRITICAL TEXTOLOGICAL ANALYSIS OF CHAPTER 7 («ATHĀSANĀNI»)

Haṭhatattvakaumudī · chapter 7 · Athāsanāni · fifteen āsanas

INTRODUCTORY SECTION: METHODOLOGY, TRANSCENDENCE OF DUALITY, AND THE TRIAD OF PRACTICES (VERSES 1–4)

Verse 1: The Body as an Instrument and Conquering the Pairs of Opposites

शरीरसाधनत्वाच्च द्वन्द्वानों सहनाच्च यत् ।तस्मात् पूर्वं समाचार्यमासनं दौर्बल्यापहम्¹ ॥ १ ॥

śarīrasādhanatvāc ca dvandvānāṃ sahanāc ca yat ।tasmāt pūrvaṃ samācāryam āsanaṃ daurbalyāpaham¹ ॥ 1 ॥

Word-by-Word Grammatical Analysis

śarīrasādhanatvāt [śarīra-sādhana-tva-]
noun, neuter, ablative of cause, sing. (compound: śarīra ["body"] + sādhana ["means, instrument"] + abstract suffix -tva); sandhi before ca: -t assimilates to -c; — "because it serves as the means/instrument for [disciplining] the body";
ca
indeclinable conjunctive particle — "and, also";
dvandvānām [dvandva-]
noun, neuter, genitive plural — "of the pairs of opposites (heat and cold, pleasure and pain)";
sahanāt [sahana-]
noun, neuter, ablative of cause, sing. (from root sah ["to endure, bear"]); sandhi before ca: -t assimilates to -c; — "by virtue of enabling endurance/tolerance";
ca
indeclinable particle — "and";
yat
relative pronoun, neuter, nominative singular — "inasmuch as, since";
tasmāt
demonstrative pronoun, neuter, ablative singular — "therefore, for that reason";
pūrvam [pūrva-]
adverbial accusative / temporal adverb — "first of all, initially, prior to all else";
samācāryam [sam-ā-cārya-]
gerundive (future passive participle / obligative) from verb sam-ā-car (root car with prefixes sam- and ā-), neuter, nominative singular — "ought to be practiced / must be performed";
āsanam [āsana-]
noun, neuter, nominative singular — "seat, posture (āsana)";
daurbalyāpaham [daurbalya-apaha-]
compound adjective, neuter, nominative singular (compound: daurbalya ["debility, weakness"] + apaha ["dispelling, destroying"] from root han with prefix apa-) — "that which eradicates debility / removes bodily weakness".

Literal Translation

Inasmuch as [it] serves as the instrument of the body and because of [its enabling] endurance of the pairs of opposites, therefore posture (āsana), which removes debility, should be practiced first of all.

Literary Translation

Inasmuch as posture (āsana) disciplines the body as an indispensable vehicle and bestows the fortitude to withstand the dualities of existence (heat and cold, pleasure and sorrow), it must be mastered prior to all other disciplines as that sovereign practice which eradicates bodily debility.

Textological Commentary and Critical Apparatus

  • Footnote 1 to Verse 1:
  • In the primary witness «a» (manuscript 4235, Ujjain), the reading is: daurbalyāpaham (daurbalyāpaham — "dispelling debility / destroying weakness").
  • In transcript «b» (manuscript 13247, Lonavla), Doctor Harshe records: dārḍhyāvaham (dārḍhyāvaham — "bringing firmness / stability").
  • Evaluation: Reading «b» (dārḍhyāvaham) represents an editorial normalization influenced by the famous dictum of the Haṭhapradīpikā («Haṭhapradīpikā» 1.17: kuryāt tad āsanaṃ sthairyam ārogyaṃ cāṅgalāghavam), where firmness (dārḍhya) is declared the fruit of āsana. However, the reading of «a» (daurbalyāpaham) is the lectio difficilior and entirely aligns with Sundaradeva's identity as a clinical physician (vaidya): for him, āsana is primarily a therapeutic remedy that eliminates pathological weakness (daurbalya). The reading of witness «a» is rightfully retained as authentic.

Verse 2: The Principle of the "Seed State" and Detachment from the Physical Body

बीजभावं गते चास्मिन् कायवैराग्यसंभवात् ।उपायानामनेकत्वानासनं पूर्वमुच्यते ॥ २ ॥

bījabhāvaṃ gate cāsmin kāyavairāgyasaṃbhavāt ।upāyānām anekatvād āsanaṃ pūrvam ucyate ॥ 2 ॥

Word-by-Word Grammatical Analysis

bījabhāvam [bīja-bhāva-]
noun, masculine, accusative singular (compound: bīja ["seed, causal origin"] + bhāva ["state, condition"]) — "into the state of a seed / seminal cause [of yoga]";
gate [gata-]
past passive participle of root gam ["to go, attain"], neuter, locative singular (forming a locative absolute with asmin: gate cāsmin — "and when this [body] has attained");
ca
indeclinable conjunction — "and";
asmin [idam-]
demonstrative pronoun, neuter, locative singular — "in this [body]";
kāyavairāgyasaṃbhavāt [kāya-vairāgya-sambhava-]
noun, masculine/neuter, ablative of cause, sing. (compound: kāya ["body"] + vairāgya ["dispassion, detachment"] + sambhava ["arising, origin"]) — "owing to the arising of dispassion toward the physical body";
upāyānām [upāya-]
noun, masculine, genitive plural — "among [all the various] means and methods";
anekatvāt [an-eka-tva-]
noun, neuter, ablative of cause, sing. — "by reason of their manifoldness / multiplicity"; sandhi before vowel: -t voices to -d;
āsanam [āsana-]
noun, neuter, nominative singular — "posture (āsana)";
pūrvam [pūrva-]
adverb — "first, foremost, prior";
ucyate [vac]
verb, present passive indicative, 3rd person singular — "is declared, is proclaimed".

Literal Translation

And when this [body] has attained the state of a seed through the arising of dispassion toward the body, among the multiplicity of means, posture (āsana) is declared first.

Literary Translation

When, through the dawn of detachment from the transient physical sheath, the body is reduced to a pure seminal foundation, posture (āsana) is declared the foremost among all the manifold methods of yoga.

Textological Commentary and Critical Apparatus

  • No textual variants are recorded between manuscripts «a» and «b» for Verse 2. The reading is stable.
  • Semantic Note: The term bījabhāva denotes the causal, primordial state wherein the body's gross identification is sublimated into a pure seed-matrix for spiritual realization.

Verse 3: The Triad of Practices for Subduing the Vital Breath

साधनत्रयमिदं मरुज्जये प्रोदितं हि मितजग्धिविष्टरे² ।शक्तिचालमथ साधनयुग्मं नाभवायुधृति नाडिचालनम्³ ॥ ३ ॥

sādhanatrayam idaṃ marujjaye proditaṃ hi mitajagdhiviṣṭare² ।śakticālam atha sādhanayugmaṃ nābhivāyudhṛti nāḍicālanam³ ॥ 3 ॥

Word-by-Word Grammatical Analysis

sādhanatrayam [sādhana-traya-]
noun, neuter, nominative singular (compound: sādhana ["means, practice"] + traya ["triad"]) — "this triad of practices";
idam [idam-]
demonstrative pronoun, neuter, nominative singular — "this";
marujjaye [marud-jaya-]
noun, masculine, locative singular (compound: marut ["wind, prāṇa"] + jaya ["conquest, subdual"]; sandhi: -t assimilates to -j- before j-); — "in the conquest of the vital breath (prāṇa)";
proditam [pra-udita-]
past passive participle of root vad with prefix pra- (sandhi: pra + uditam = proditam), neuter, nominative singular — "declared, set forth";
hi
emphatic/causal particle — "indeed, verily, for";
mitajagdhiviṣṭare [mita-jagdhi-viṣṭara-]
noun, masculine/neuter, locative singular (dvandva/tatpuruṣa compound: mita ["measured, moderate"] + jagdhi ["eating, nourishment", from root jakṣ] + viṣṭara ["seat, posture, āsana"]) — "in moderate eating and [proper] seating (āsana)";
śakticālam [śakti-cāla-]
noun, neuter, nominative singular (compound: śakti ["power, kuṇḍalinī"] + cāla ["movement, arousal"]) — "the arousing of the power (kuṇḍalinī)";
atha
indeclinable sequential particle — "then, furthermore";
sādhanayugmam [sādhana-yugma-]
noun, neuter, nominative singular (compound: sādhana ["practice"] + yugma ["pair, couple"]) — "the pair of practices";
nābhivāyudhṛti [nābhi-vāyu-dhṛti-]
noun, feminine, nominative singular (compound: nābhi ["navel"] + vāyu ["vital breath"] + dhṛti ["retention, holding"]) — "the retention of the vital breath in the navel center";
nāḍicālanam [nāḍi-cālana-]
noun, neuter, nominative singular (compound: nāḍi ["channel"] + cālana ["movement, purification"]) — "the circulation/purification through the subtle channels (nāḍīs)".

Literal Translation

This triad of practices is indeed declared in the conquest of the breath under the condition of moderate diet and [proper] seat: the arousing of the power (śakti-cāla), and then the pair of practices: retention of the breath in the navel (nābhi-vāyu-dhṛti) and circulation through the channels (nāḍi-cālana).

Literary Translation

Verily, for the subjugation of the vital breath (prāṇa) under the conditions of moderate diet and firm seating (āsana), a triad of practices is proclaimed: the awakening of the dormant power (śakticālana), followed by the complementary pair — retention of breath at the navel center and the purifying circulation through the energetic channels (nāḍīs).

Textological Commentary and Critical Apparatus

  • Footnote 2 to «mitajagdhiviṣṭare»:
  • In witness «a»: mitajagdhiviṣṭare (mitajagdhiviṣṭare — from mita-jagdhi ["moderate eating"] + viṣṭara ["seat/āsana"]).
  • In witness «b»: mitajagdhiviṣṭaye (mitajagdhiviṣṭaye).
  • Evaluation: The ending -viṣṭaye in «b» is an undeniable scribal blunder (corruption). The scribe misread the Devanāgarī consonant ra (र) as ya (य), producing an unintelligible pseudo-word. The reading of «a» is fully authentic.
  • Footnote 3 to «nāḍicālanam»:
  • In witness «a»: nāḍicālanam (nāḍicālanam) with short vowel -i-.
  • In witness «b»: nāḍīcālanam (nāḍīcālanam) with standardized long -ī-.
  • Evaluation: The shortening of the feminine stem vowel in compound formation is a well-attested metric license in verse treatises of Haṭha yoga to preserve the anubhṣṭubh meter. Manuscript «b» represents a grammatical hypercorrection.

Verse 4: The Five Limbs of Haṭha Yoga and the Primacy of Āsanas

हठस्याङ्गानि पञ्चेदं साधनानि प्रचक्षते ।पूर्वं वायुजयादासनं तद् युक्त्या समाचरेत्⁴ ॥ ४ ॥

haṭhasyāṅgāni pañcedaṃ sādhanāni pracakṣate ।pūrvaṃ vāyujayād āsanaṃ tad yuktyā samācaret⁴ ॥ 4 ॥

Literal Translation

The five limbs of Haṭha, these they declare to be the practical means. Prior to the conquest of the breath, therefore, one should methodically practice posture (āsana).

Literary Translation

The masters proclaim that the system of Haṭha yoga comprises five practical limbs. Therefore, prior to undertaking the conquest of the vital breath (prāṇāyāma), the practitioner must methodically and skillfully master posture (āsana).

Textological Commentary and Critical Apparatus

  • Footnote 4 to «samācaret»:
  • In witness «a»: the regular 3rd person singular active optative samācaret (samācaret).
  • In witness «b»: the scribal misreading samācareta (samācareta), incorrectly adding a terminal vowel or confusing parasmaipada with ātmanepada. Witness «a» is grammatically correct.
  • The Fivefold Division of Haṭha Yoga: Sundaradeva's articulation of pañcāṅga-haṭha synthesizes the classical continuum: 1) āsana, 2) prāṇāyāma, 3) mudrā, 4) nādānusandhāna (laya), and 5) rājayoga (samādhi).
