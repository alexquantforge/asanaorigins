SECTION III. COMPREHENSIVE COMPARATIVE SYNTHESIS TABLE OF THE 15 ĀSANAS

Haṭhatattvakaumudī

№Āsana Name (IAST)Sanskrit NameVersesKey Biomechanical DominantPathophysiological and Therapeutic Efficacy according to Sundaradeva
1Dhanurāsanaधनुरासनम्5Seizing toes with hands and pulling leg back toward the ear like an archer's bowstringPurification of subtle energetic channels (nāḍīśuddhi), opening of pelvic joints, expansion of thoracic cavity
2Kukkuṭāsanaकुक्कुटासनम्6Threading arms between thighs and calves in Padmāsana; balancing aloft on palmsStrengthens pectoral and shoulder girdle; creates deep core abdominal compression; stimulates visceral digestive fire
3Matsyendrapīṭhaमत्स्येन्द्रपीठम्7–8Deep spiral spinal rotation with foot placed against the opposite thigh baseShatters host of fierce diseases like a divine missile; fans jaṭharāgni; awakens kuṇḍalinī; preserves lunar nectar (amṛta)
4Mayūrāsanaमयूरासनम्9–10Balancing on elbows pressed beside the navel; body extended horizontally like a staffCures spleen tumors (gulma) and dropsy (udara); subdues vitiated doṣas; incinerates indiscriminate food and digests lethal kālakūṭa poison
5Śavāsanaशवासनम्11–13Complete supine relaxation on the floor like an unmoving corpseInstantly dissolves spasmodic vātagaṇḍa (vātic knots/tumors) in the chest; dispels systemic exhaustion; readies body for prāṇāyāma
6Siṃhāsanaसिंहासनम्14–16Ankles crossed beneath scrotum; hands on knees; jaws agape; gazing at nose-tip (nāsāgradṛṣṭi)Subdues aggravated vāta; induces spontaneous synergistic engagement of the three locks (mūla-, uḍḍīyāna-, jālandhara-bandh a)
7Bhadrāsanaभद्रासनम्17Soles joined beneath scrotum beside perineum; hands firmly clasping feetEradicates all diseases; purges endogenous toxic metabolic waste; pacifies aggravated kapha and vāta
8Vīrāsanaवीरासनम्18–19One foot placed upon opposite thigh near knee with spine held perfectly verticalBestows fulfillment of all desires (sarvakāmaprada) ; builds resolute fortitude (dhṛti); prepares mind for contemplating the Ātman and gaining jñāna
9Kūrmāsanaकूर्मासनम्20Ankles crossed and pressed firmly against the anal sphincterRadically cures all 80 classical disorders of the vāta humor (sarvavātarugāpa ha) by regulating apāna-vāyu
10Prasāritāsanaप्रसारितासनम्21Legs extended wide and flat upon the floor; hands grasping feetStretches posterior myofascial and meridian lines; establishes an unshakable foundation for advanced Rāja Yoga
11Padmāsanaपद्मासनम्22–28Feet crossed soles-upward on opposite thighs; hands cupped; nabhomudrā; jālandharabandhaDestroys all diseases; dynamically balances prāṇa and apāna (reversing apāna upward and prāṇa downward); forces vital breath into suṣumnā
12Baddhapadmāsanaबद्धपद्मासनम्29–32Cross-locking hands behind the back to clasp opposite big toes in PadmāsanaConquers chronic mixed fevers of vāta and kapha (vātakaphajvarāpa ha); eliminates deep-seated toxins; corroborated by Yogacandrikā
13Siddhāsana / Yonyāsanaसिद्धासनम् / योन्यासनम्33–40Heel pressed into perineum (yonisthāna); upper heel above pubic bone; śāmbhavī- or nāsāgradṛṣṭiQueen of all āsanas (peerless like the holy Gaṅgā among rivers); absolves all sins without need for strenuous śīrṣāsana; grants mastery in 1 year
14Paścimatānāsanaपश्चिमाता Liberator41–43Seated forward bend with legs extended; forehead resting on knees; toes claspedForces breath into suṣumnā; endows yogin with splendor of 10 million suns; reignites extinguished gastric fire; eradicates vāta
15Svastikāsanaस्वस्तिकासनम्44–45Soles tucked symmetrically between opposite thighs and calves with upright spineIntegrates vital currents (prāṇasya saṃdhānadam); instantly destroys hosts of diseases; kindles visceral belly flame; grants eternal peace
