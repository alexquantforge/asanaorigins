Opening Instruction and the List of the Eight Postures (verses 1–4)

Dharmaputrikā · chapter 2 · Āsanaprakaraṇam · eight postures

1

अष्टभिः साधनैरेभिश्चित्तं कायं च यत्नतः ।शोधयित्वा ततो योगी योगाभ्यासं समाचरेत्॥१॥

aṣṭabhiḥ sādhanair ebhiś cittaṃ kāyaṃ ca yatnataḥ |śodhayitvā tato yogī yogābhyāsaṃ samācaret ||1||

ашт̣абхих̣ сāдханаир ебхиш́ читтам̣ кāйам̣ ча йатнатах̣ |ш́одхайитвā тато йогӣ йогāбхйāсам̣ самāчарет ||1||

2

इष्टदेवं नमस्कृत्योपाध्यायं चैव यत्नतः ।आतिष्ठेत्प्राङ्मुखो भूत्वा शुचिर्योगी विशेषतः ॥२॥

iṣṭadevaṃ namaskṛtyopādhyāyaṃ caiva yatnataḥ |ātiṣṭhet prāṅmukho bhūtvā śucir yogī viśeṣataḥ ||2||

ишт̣адевам̣ намаскр̣тйопāдхйāйам̣ чаива йатнатах̣ |āтишт̣хет прāн̇мукхо бхӯтвā ш́учир йогӣ виш́ешатах̣ ||2||

3

पद्मस्वस्तिकपीठानि स्थलमञ्जलिकासनं।अर्धचन्द्रञ्च दण्डञ्च सर्वतोभद्रमेव च ॥३॥

padmasvastikapīṭhāni sthalam añjalikāsanam |ardhacandrañ ca daṇḍañ ca sarvatobhadram eva ca ||3||

падмасвастикапӣт̣хāни стхалам ан̃джаликāсанам |ардхачандран̃ ча дан̣д̣ан̃ ча сарватобхадрам ева ча ||3||

4

एवं नामभिरष्टाभिरासनप्रकरणं स्मृतं।तेषां संस्थानभेदन्तु प्रवक्ष्यामि यथाक्रमं॥४॥

evaṃ nāmabhir aṣṭābhir āsanaprakaraṇaṃ smṛtam |teṣāṃ saṃsthānabhedan tu pravakṣyāmi yathākramam ||4||

евам̣ нāмабхир ашт̣āбхир āсанапракаран̣ам̣ смр̣там |тешāм̣ сам̣стхāнабхедан ту правакшйāми йатхāкрамам ||4||

Literal Translation

Having diligently purified the mind and the body by these eight means, the yogin should then undertake the practice of yoga. Having carefully paid homage to the chosen deity and to the preceptor, the yogin, having become pure and facing east, should in particular assume [the postures]. The lotus, the svastika, the thrones, the mound, the joined-hands posture, the half-moon, the staff, and the all-auspicious: thus the section on the postures is handed down through eight names; I will explain their different forms in due succession.

Literary Translation

After purifying the mind and the body diligently by the eight means [that have just been mentioned], the yogin then undertakes the repeated practice which aims at yoga. (1) Having paid homage to [his] chosen deity and to [his] preceptor carefully, facing East, the yogin, after he has become pure, should assume [the following postures] in particular: (2) the lotus, the svastika, the throne, the mound, the joined-hands-posture, and the half-moon, the staff and the all-auspicious. (3) Thus, the subject relating to the postures is taught through [these] eight names; I will explain their different forms, in due succession. (4)

The "eight means" (aṣṭau sādhanāni) refer to the eight auxiliary practices described in the preceding chapter of the Dharmaputrikā; they are the preliminary purification required before postural practice.

Facing east (prāṅmukha) and ritual purity (śuci) are standard preliminary conditions for yogic and ritual practice.

In verse 3 the first three postures are grouped in a neuter plural compound (padma-svastika-pīṭhāni), after which the remaining five are named individually.

In verse 4 the edition reads saṃsthānabhedan tu (with a final -n); this is an editorial realization of the anusvāra before t-, for saṃsthānabhedaṃ tu ("the different forms").
