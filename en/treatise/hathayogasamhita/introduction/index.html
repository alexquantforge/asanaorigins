Chapter 2: Āsana-prakaraṇa — The Section on Postures

Haṭhayogasaṃhitā · chapter 2 · Āsana-prakaraṇa · thirty-three āsanas · verses 2.1–2.52

Verse 2.1 — Definition of Āsana

Verses: 2.1

2.1

अभ्यासाद्यस्य देहोऽयं योगौपयिकतां व्रजेत् । मनश्च स्थिरतामेति प्रोच्यते तदिहाऽऽसनम् ॥ १ ॥

abhyāsādyasya deho'yaṃ yogaupayikatāṃ vrajet | manaśca sthiratāmeti procyate tadihā''sanam || 1 ||

абхйāсāдйасйа дехо'йам̣ йогаупайикатāм̣ враджет | манаш́ча стхиратāмети прочйате тадихā''санам || 1 ||

Hindi commentary (corrected text)

जिस तरह बैठने के अभ्यास से यह शरीर योगोपयोगी होता है और मन स्थिर होता है उसको आसन कहते हैं।

Word-by-word analysis

abhyāsāt — (ablative singular) from practice; yasya — (genitive singular) which; dehaḥ — (nominative singular) body; ayam — (nominative singular) this; yoga-aupayikatāṃ — (accusative singular) fitness for yoga; vrajet — (optative 3 singular) can gain; manaḥ — (nominative singular) mind; ca — And; sthiratām — (accusative singular) stability; eti — (present 3 singular) comes/reaches; procyate — (passive present 3 singular) is called; tat — That; iha — Here; āsanam — (nominative singular) āsana.

Translation

“That by the practice of which the body becomes fit for yoga and the mind becomes stable is here called Āsana.”

English translation of the Hindi commentary

“The practice of sitting by which this body becomes useful for yoga and the mind becomes steady is called āsana.”

Verse 2.2 — The Number of Āsanas

Verses: 2.2

2.2

आसनानि समस्तानि यावत्यो जीवयोनयः । चतुरशीतिलक्षाणि शिवेन कथितानि तु ॥ २ ॥

āsanāni samastāni yāvatyo jīvayonayaḥ | caturaśītilakṣāṇi śivena kathitāni tu || 2 ||

āсанāни самастāни йāватйо джӣвайонайах̣ | чатураш́ӣтилакшāн̣и ш́ивена катхитāни ту || 2 ||

Hindi commentary (corrected text)

जितनी योनि के प्राणी हैं आसनों की संख्या भी उतनी ही जानना उचित है, देवादिदेव महादेव ने चौरासी लक्ष आसनों का वर्णन किया था।

Word-by-word analysis

āsanāni samastāni — (nominative plural) all āsanas; yāvatyaḥ — (nominative plural) how much; jīva-yonayaḥ — species of living things; caturaśīti-lakṣāṇi — 84 lakh (8,400,000); śivena — (instrumental) Śiva; kathitāni — (participle) told/described; tu — same.

Translation

“There are as many āsanas as there are types of living beings. Verily, 84 lakh (8,400,000) [āsanas] have been described by Śiva.”

English translation of the Hindi commentary

“As many types of living beings exist, the same number of āsanas should be known. 84 lakh āsanas have been described by the God of Gods Mahādeva.”

Verse 2.3 — The Most Important Āsanas

Verses: 2.3

2.3

तेषां मध्ये विशिष्टानि षोडशोनं शतं कृतम् । आसनानि त्रयस्त्रिंशन्मर्त्यलोके शुभानि वै ॥ ३ ॥

teṣāṃ madhye viśiṣṭāni ṣoḍaśonaṃ śataṃ kṛtam | āsanāni trayastriṃśanmartyaloke śubhāni vai || 3 ||

тешāм̣ мадхйе виш́ишт̣āни шод̣аш́онам̣ ш́атам̣ кр̣там | āсанāни трайастрим̣ш́анмартйалоке ш́убхāни ваи || 3 ||

Hindi commentary (corrected text)

उनमं से चौरासी आसन सबसे श्रेष्ठ हैं; और उन चौरासियों मं से मानवलोक मं तैतीस आसन कल्याण को देने वाले हैं।

Word-by-word analysis

teṣāṃ madhye — among them; viśiṣṭāni — (nominative plural) outstanding/most important; ṣoḍaśa-ūnaṃ śataṃ — one hundred to sixteen (84); kṛtam — selected; āsanāni — āsanas; trayas-triṃśat — thirty three; martya-loke — in the mortal world; śubhāni — (nominative plural) favorable; vai — true.

Translation

“Among them, eighty-four are considered the most important. [Of these] thirty-three āsanas are truly auspicious in the mortal world.”

English translation of the Hindi commentary

“Among them, 84 āsanas are the best; and out of these 84 in the world of people, 33 āsanas bring good.”

Verse 2.4-6 — The Place of Practice: The Maṭha

Verses: 2.4-6

2.4-6

सुराज्ये धार्मिके देशे सुभिक्षे निरुपद्रवे । धनुःप्रमाणपर्यन्तं शिलाग्निजलवर्जिते ॥ ४ ॥ एकान्ते मठिकामध्ये स्थातव्यं हठयोगिना ॥ ५ ॥ अल्पद्वारमरन्ध्रगर्तविवरं नात्युच्चनीचायतं सम्यग्गोमयसान्द्रलिप्तममलं निःशेषजन्तूज्झितम् । बाह्ये मण्डपवेदिकूपरुचिरं प्राकारसंवेष्टितं प्रोक्तं योगमठस्य लक्षणमिदं सिद्धैर्हठाभ्यासिभिः ॥ ६ ॥

surājye dhārmike deśe subhikṣe nirupadrave | dhanuḥpramāṇaparyantaṃ śilāgnijalavarjite || 4 || ekānte maṭhikāmadhye sthātavyaṃ haṭhayoginā || 5 || alpadvāramarandhragartavivaraṃ nātyuccanīcāyataṃ samyaggomayasāndraliptamamalaṃ niḥśeṣajantūjjhitam | bāhye maṇḍapavedikūparuciraṃ prākārasaṃveṣṭitaṃ proktaṃ yogamaṭhasya lakṣaṇamidaṃ siddhairhaṭhābhyāsibhiḥ || 6 ||

сурāджйе дхāрмике деш́е субхикше нирупадраве | дханух̣прамāн̣апарйантам̣ ш́илāгниджалаварджите || 4 || екāнте мат̣хикāмадхйе стхāтавйам̣ хат̣хайогинā || 5 || алпадвāрамарандхрагартавиварам̣ нāтйуччанӣчāйатам̣ самйаггомайасāндралиптамамалам̣ них̣ш́ешаджантӯджджхитам | бāхйе ман̣д̣апаведикӯпаручирам̣ прāкāрасам̣вешт̣итам̣ проктам̣ йогамат̣хасйа лакшан̣амидам̣ сиддхаирхат̣хāбхйāсибхих̣ || 6 ||

Hindi commentary (corrected text)

जहां सुराज्य हो, जो देश धार्मिक हो, जहां सुभिक्ष रहे, जिस देश मं किसी प्रकार का उपद्रव न रहे वहां शिला, अग्नि और जल से धनुः प्रमाण परिमित दूर पर रहकर एकान्त स्थान मं छोटी सी मठिका बनाकर योगी को योग साधन करना उचित है। योग साधन गृह मं छोटा द्वार होना उचित है, वह घर छेद और बिल आदि से रहित हो, वह न तो बहुत ऊंचा हो और न बहुत नीचा, गोमय से लिपा हुआ हो, और सब प्रकार के कीटों से रहित हो तब ही वह साधन उपयोगी होगा। उस मठ के बाहर एक मण्डप, एक वेदी और एक कूप रहना उचित है। ऐसा वृक्ष आदि से रमणीय स्थान प्राकार द्वारा वेष्टित होने से वह योगाभ्यास के उपयोगी होता है और योगियों को सिद्धि दान कर सकता है।

Word-by-word analysis

su-rājye — in the good kingdom; dhārmike deśe — in a righteous country; su-bhikṣe — where is it easy to get food; nirupadrave — where there are no worries; dhanuḥ-pramāṇa-paryantaṃ — at a distance of a bow's length; śilā-agni-jala-varjite — free from stones, fire and water; ekānte — in solitude; maṭhikā-madhye — inside the hut; sthātavyaṃ — should stay; haṭha-yoginā — Haṭhayoga; alpa-dvāram — with a small door; arandhra-garta-vivaraṃ — without cracks, holes or cavities; na-ati-ucca-nīca-āyataṃ — not too high, not low and not too long; samyak-gomaya-sāndra-liptam — well coated with thick cow dung; amalaṃ — clean; niḥśeṣa-jantu-ujjhitam — free from all insects; bāhye — outside; maṇḍapa-vedi-kūpa-ruciraṃ — decorated with a canopy, altar and well; prākāra-saṃveṣṭitaṃ — surrounded by a wall/fence; proktaṃ — described; yoga-maṭhasya lakṣaṇam — sign of a yogic abode; idam — This; siddhaiḥ — siddhas; haṭha-abhyāsibhiḥ — practicing Haṭhayoga.

Translation

“In a good kingdom, in a righteous country, where food is easy to obtain and there is no trouble. In a secluded place, free from stones, fire and dampness within a bow's length (around), a haṭhayogin should live in a small hut. [The hut should be] with a small door, without cracks, holes or cavities, not too high and not too low. It should be well coated with thick cow dung, clean and completely free from insects. Outside - it is decorated with a canopy, a raised platform (altar) and a well, and is surrounded by a fence. These are the signs of the yogic abode (maṭha) described by the siddhas who practice Haṭhayoga.”

English translation of the Hindi commentary

“Where there is good government, the country is religious, where there is an abundance of food and there is no disturbance - there, in a secluded place, at a distance of a bow's length from rocks, fire and water, a yogi should build a small hut and practice yoga. The house for yoga should have a small door, the house should be without holes and holes, not too high and not too low, covered with dung and free from all types of insects - only then is it suitable for practice. Outside this maṭha there should be a mandapa (canopy), vedi (altar) and a well. Such a place, pleasant because of trees and other things, surrounded by a wall, is beneficial for yogabhyasa and can bestow siddhis on yogis.”

Verse 2.7 — Beginning the Practice

Verses: 2.7

2.7

एवंविधे मठे स्थित्वा सर्वचिन्ताविवर्जितः । गुरूपदिष्टमार्गेण योगमेवं समभ्यसेत् ॥ ७ ॥

evamvidhe maṭhe sthitvā sarvacintāvivarjitaḥ | gurūpadiṣṭamārgeṇa yogamevaṃ samabhyaset || 7 ||

евамвидхе мат̣хе стхитвā сарвачинтāвиварджитах̣ | гурӯпадишт̣амāрген̣а йогамевам̣ самабхйасет || 7 ||

Hindi commentary (corrected text)

इस प्रकार के मठ मं स्थित होकर सब प्रकार की चिन्ताओं को त्याग करके गुरु उपदिष्ट साधन अनुसार अभ्यास करना मुमुक्षु को उचित है।

Word-by-word analysis

evam-vidhe — in this kind; maṭhe — monastery; sthitvā — being; sarva-cintā-vivarjitaḥ — throwing away all worries; guru-upadiṣṭa-mārgeṇa — the path indicated by the guru; yogam — yoga; evam — So; samabhyaset — should be practiced.

Translation

“Being in such an abode, having given up all worries, one should practice yoga in the way prescribed by the Guru.”

English translation of the Hindi commentary

"Being in such a maṭha, abandoning all kinds of worries, the mumukṣu (seeker of liberation) should practice according to the method prescribed by the guru."
