Haṭha yoga · Sanskrit anthology of 1363 · printed edition: Peterson, Bombay Sanskrit Series XXXVII (1888) · verses 4375–4437

Abstract

This critical monograph provides an exhaustive philological and historical study of the dating of the celebrated 14th-century Sanskrit anthology, the Śārṅgadharapaddhati (1363 CE), followed by a comprehensive scholarly presentation of its complete corpus of āsana descriptions.

In the historiography of Indian physical and contemplative traditions, the Śārṅgadharapaddhati occupies a pivotal position: it is the earliest securely and explicitly dated encyclopedic Sanskrit text to transmit the bodily and breath techniques of Haṭhayoga prior to the compilation of the Haṭhapradīpikā (c. 1400–1450 CE). Because Śārṅgadhara incorporated verbatim citations from the Dattātreyayogaśāstra into Chapters 157 and 158, the secure date of his work (1363 CE) establishes an absolute chronological benchmark (terminus ante quem) for the emergence and widespread North Indian circulation of systematic Haṭhayoga.
