Ardhapadmāsana

Ardhapadmāsana

Yoga Kaustubha

Dating of the text

  • The title page of the editionŚaka 1811; Saṃvat 1946not equated with a Gregorian yearBasis of the date (in the volume’s own words)The title page gives Śaka 1811 and Saṃvat 1946. Without further calendar clarification, these dates are not equated here with a single Gregorian year.

The conversion is our arithmetic, not the volume’s words. Śaka 1811: 1811 + 78 = 1889 for the months from the start of the year to December, and 1811 + 79 = 1890 for January–February. Saṃvat 1946: 1946 − 57 = 1889 and 1946 − 56 = 1890, assuming the same start of the year; in Gujarat the Vikrama saṃvat counted from Caitra is the usual reckoning, but the volume does not say so. Both dates give the same span, March 1889 to March 1890, and differ by exactly 135 years, as the eras themselves do: the title page does not contradict itself. But neither month nor day is printed on it, so no single year can be named.

Printed source: pages 60–61 (PDF page number is printed page plus 1).

જમણા પગની પાની બેસીને ડાબા સાથળના મૂળમાં રાખવી, ને ડાબા પગની પાની જમણા સાથળના મૂળમાં રાખવી તે અર્ધપદ્માસન કિંવા મુક્તપદ્માસન કહેવાય છે.

jamaṇā paganī pānī besīne ḍābā sāthaḷanā mūḷamāṁ rākhavī, ne ḍābā paganī pānī jamaṇā sāthaḷanā mūḷamāṁ rākhavī te ardhapadmāsana kiṁvā muktapadmāsana kahevāya che.

джаман̣ā паганӣ пāнӣ бесӣне д̣āбā сāтхал̣анā мӯл̣амāм̣ рāкхавӣ, не д̣āбā паганӣ пāнӣ джаман̣ā сāтхал̣анā мӯл̣амāм̣ рāкхавӣ те ардхападмāсана ким̣вā муктападмāсана кахевāйа чхе.

Word-by-word analysis

jamaṇā paganī pānī — the right heel; besīne — sitting; ḍābā sāthaḷanā mūḷamāṁ rākhavī — place at the root of the left thigh; ne ḍābā paganī pānī — and the left heel; jamaṇā sāthaḷanā mūḷamāṁ rākhavī — place at the root of the right thigh; te ardhapadmāsana kiṁvā muktapadmāsana kahevāya che — this is called Ardhapadmāsana or Muktapadmāsana. The placement of besīne in the printed sentence is awkward.

Literal translation (our interlinear)

The heel of the right leg, sitting, at the root of the left thigh to place, and the heel of the left leg at the root of the right thigh to place — this is called Ardhapadmāsana or Muktapadmāsana.

Literary translation (ours)

Sitting, place the heel of the right foot at the base of the left thigh and the heel of the left foot at the base of the right thigh. This is called Ardhapadmāsana or Muktapadmāsana. The printed wording is syntactically awkward; the position of “sitting” here is an interpretation.

Additional passage

ડાબા પગની પાની જમણા સાથળના મૂળમાં ભરાવીને, ને જમણા પગની પાની ડાબા સાથળના મૂળમાં ભરાવીને બેસવાથી પણ એ આસન થઈ શકે છે.

ḍābā paganī pānī jamaṇā sāthaḷanā mūḷamāṃ bharāvīne, ne jamaṇā paganī pānī ḍābā sāthaḷanā mūḷamāṃ bharāvīne besavāthī paṇa e āsana thaī śake che.

д̣āбā паганӣ пāнӣ джаман̣ā сāтхал̣анā мӯл̣амāм̣ бхарāвӣне, не джаман̣ā паганӣ пāнӣ д̣āбā сāтхал̣анā мӯл̣амāм̣ бхарāвӣне бесавāтхӣ пан̣а е āсана тхаӣ ш́аке чхе.

Word-by-word analysis (ours)

ḍābā paganī pānī — the heel of the left leg; jamaṇā sāthaḷanā mūḷamāṃ — at the base of the right thigh; bharāvīne — having inserted; ne — and; jamaṇā paganī pānī — the heel of the right leg; ḍābā sāthaḷanā mūḷamāṃ — at the base of the left thigh; bharāvīne — having inserted; besavāthī — from sitting; paṇa — too; e āsana — this posture; thaī śake che — can come about.

Literal translation (our interlinear)

This posture can also come about by sitting after inserting the heel of the left leg at the base of the right thigh, and inserting the heel of the right leg at the base of the left thigh.

Additional passage — English (ours)

The posture can be made by placing the left heel at the base of the right thigh and the right heel at the base of the left thigh and sitting. The preceding wording remains syntactically uncertain.

Textual note

Printed pages 60–61: the sequence concerning sitting and the right heel is syntactically difficult. The translation remains tentative; the printed text also gives an alternative arrangement on the following page.
